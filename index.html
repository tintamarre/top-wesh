How to play

  • Drag left and right (or ← →, A D) to steer your squad. It runs and shoots by itself.
  • Walk through blue and yellow gates to grow your squad. Avoid the red ones, or shoot them until they turn blue.
  • In the three-lane sections, pick your lane: a row of +1, the red horde, or a barrier to break with big + gates behind it.
  • Shoot jars to free prisoners, and barrels and ice crates for a gun. When two guns stand side by side, the one you break is the one you get: Shotgun and Flamer are short and wide, Rifle and Laser go through the ranks, Rockets explode.
  • Glowing boxes: ⚡ rage doubles your fire, 🛡️ shield stops every loss for a few seconds, 💣 airstrike clears the road.
  • Step around spike strips, saw blades, closing walls, rolling iron balls (bullets bounce off) and the red orbs fired by purple marksmen.
  • Red soldiers trade one for one. Shield bearers stop bullets, bombers blow up their friends when shot early, orange runners are fast.
  • Three bosses: the Crusher smashes the ground, the Blaster fires fans of orbs, the Titan does both. Below half health they get angry.
  • Coins lie on the road, often where it is risky. After every third level a coin rush has nothing but coins and traps.
  • Finish with 80% of your soldiers for ★★★. The first time you clear a level you pick a perk that stays for good.
  • Spend your coins on Recruits, Firepower and Fire rate, and on heroes: the Medic revives, the Sniper picks off the dangerous ones, the Tank takes a heavy blow.
