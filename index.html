How to play

  • You lead a gang of teenagers. Drag left and right (or ← →, A D) to steer it. It runs and shoots by itself, and nobody gets hurt: a soaked grown-up just goes home.
  • Press WESH! (or Space) and the gang shouts: grown-ups are thrown back and stay dazed, chalk flies back at whoever threw it, homework balls roll off the road, and a boss loses the blow it was preparing. Then it takes 6 seconds to get your breath back.
  • From level 2 the Mum follows with her vacuum cleaner. She is slower than you, but each time the gang stalls in front of a crowd or a jar she catches up and vacuums up the last rows. Break through fast, step aside, or shout to send her back. She goes home when the boss shows up.
  • Walk through blue and yellow gates to call more friends. Avoid the red ones, or shoot them until they turn blue. A gate counts as soon as your first row reaches it.
  • Shoot jars to get friends out of detention, and barrels and ice crates for a new toy. When two stand side by side, the one you break is the one you get: Party popper and Deo spray are short and wide, Foam darts and Laser pointer go through the ranks, Water balloons splash.
  • Glowing boxes: 🥤 energy drink doubles your fire, 🎧 headphones let nothing reach you for a few seconds, 🔊 bass drop clears the road.
  • Step around toy bricks on the floor, giant fidget spinners, closing walls, rolling balls of homework (nothing stops homework) and the chalk thrown by the maths teachers in purple.
  • Each supervisor in red sends one friend home. Librarians hide behind a binder, surprise tests blow up in a cloud of paper, little brothers in orange are fast, gym teachers are big.
  • Three bosses: the Dad and his flip-flop, the Teacher who throws chalk in fans, the Principal who does both. Below half health they have had enough.
  • In the three-lane sections and at the forks, pick your side: easy with small gates, or guarded with a big reward.
  • Coins lie on the road, often where it is risky. After every third level a coin rush has nothing but coins and traps.
  • Finish with 80% of the gang for ★★★. The first time you clear a level you pick a perk that stays for good.
  • Spend your coins on The gang, Attitude and Sugar, and on heroes: Big sister brings friends back, The gamer tags the most annoying grown-up, Big brother takes a telling-off for you.
